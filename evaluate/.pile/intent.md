What a Datalog program derives from the relations a host holds: each relation
evaluated to its fixed point, stratum by stratum under negation.

What belongs here turns a program already read into joins, and decides what
each query answers and which programs are refused once the relations are
known: a relation nothing holds, a negation inside its own recursion, a
reference the host cannot resolve. What a program may say does not belong
here. Neither does anything about what the relations hold: a host declares
them, fills them, and resolves its references. Its README is the
specification of what a program means.

Read this pile to find out what a program derives and what evaluate refuses.
Change it when a program comes to mean something else, or when what a host
has to bring changes.
